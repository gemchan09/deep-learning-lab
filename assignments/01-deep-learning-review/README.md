# DL-LT01 — Deep Learning Literature Review

A literature review exploring the foundations of deep learning, neural network architectures, and the scientific contributions behind modern machine learning.

## Overview

This assignment examines two topics:

1. **Deep Learning Review** — an overview of deep learning, its core neural network architectures, applications, and future directions.
2. **The 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics** — a review of the foundational contributions of John J. Hopfield and Geoffrey E. Hinton to artificial neural networks and machine learning.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Deep Learning Fundamentals

- Representation learning and hierarchical feature extraction
- Supervised learning and backpropagation
- Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) and the chain rule
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)
- Distributed representations and embeddings

### 2. Applications of Deep Learning

The review discusses applications in:

- **Computer Vision:** image recognition, image segmentation, and face recognition
- **Natural Language Processing:** machine translation and language understanding
- **Speech Processing:** speech recognition and natural language generation

### 3. Foundations of Neural Networks

The second topic explores:

- **John J. Hopfield:** associative memory and Hopfield networks
- **Geoffrey E. Hinton:** Boltzmann Machines and their role in learning data representations
- Connections between statistical physics and artificial neural networks
- The development of foundational ideas that influenced modern deep learning

## Key Takeaways

- Deep learning models learn hierarchical representations from data.
- Backpropagation and gradient-based optimization provide mechanisms for adjusting neural network parameters during training.
- CNNs and RNN-based architectures address different types of data and learning tasks.
- Research connecting statistical physics with neural networks contributed to the foundations of modern machine learning.

## Learning Outcomes

Through this assignment, I explored the theoretical foundations of deep learning, reviewed major neural network architectures, and examined the relationship between physics and artificial intelligence.
